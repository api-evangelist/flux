Robin Rombach, co-founder and CEO of Black Forest Labs, addressed G7 leaders to advocate for open innovation in AI development. He emphasized that the company, a 90-person frontier AI lab based in Germany and the United States, has contributed significantly to visual generative AI research and believes open and responsible development should become the standard approach in the industry.
