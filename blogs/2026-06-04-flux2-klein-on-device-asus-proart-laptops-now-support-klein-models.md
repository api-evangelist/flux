For the first time, a FLUX model will ship on consumer hardware — FLUX.2 [klein] 4B will come preloaded and optimized on ASUS ProArt laptops featuring RTX GPUs, unveiled at Computex 2026 in Taipei, running inside ASUS's MuseTree application. The partnership with ASUS and NVIDIA aims to deliver sub-5-second image generation on an 8GB VRAM laptop without requiring internet connectivity or API dependencies.
